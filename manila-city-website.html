About the City Government of Manila

Learn about our mandate, guiding principles, and the story of the Pearl of the Orient.

Our Mission

Serbisyong Tapat, Manileño Bilang Prayoridad

To provide efficient, transparent, and responsive governance that uplifts the quality of life of every Manileño through inclusive public service, sustainable development, and the preservation of Manila's rich heritage for generations to come.

Our Vision

A World-Class, Livable, and Resilient Manila

A safe, healthy, and progressive capital city — globally competitive, culturally proud, and environmentally sustainable — where every resident enjoys equal access to opportunities, services, and a dignified quality of life.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Integrity

Upholding honesty and accountability in every transaction.

Service

Prioritizing the welfare of every Manileño above all.

Transparency

Open governance and accessible public information.

Sustainability

Building a resilient city for future generations.

Equity

Fair and inclusive access to services for all.

Excellence

Continuously improving how we serve our city.

Our Story

A City of Heritage

1571

Manila is established as the capital of the Spanish East Indies by Miguel López de Legazpi.

1901

The City of Manila is formally organized under the Philippine Commission during the American colonial period.

1945

The Battle of Manila leaves the city devastated; post-war reconstruction begins soon after.

1975

Manila becomes the seat of the National Capital Region (Metro Manila).

Today

Manila continues to modernize its services while preserving its heritage as the Pearl of the Orient.
