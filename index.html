About the City Government of Manila

Learn about our mandate, guiding principles, and the story of the Pearl of the Orient.

Our Mission

Serbisyong Tapat, Manileño Bilang Prayoridad

To provide efficient, transparent, and responsive governance that uplifts the quality of life of every Manileño through inclusive public service, sustainable development, and the preservation of Manila's rich heritage for generations to come.

Our Vision

A World-Class, Livable, and Resilient Manila

A safe, healthy, and progressive capital city — globally competitive, culturally proud, and environmentally sustainable — where every resident enjoys equal access to opportunities, services, and a dignified quality of life.

What We Stand For

Our Story

A City of Heritage

1571Founding of Manila

Manila is established as the capital of the Spanish East Indies by Miguel López de Legazpi, becoming the seat of colonial government for over three centuries.

1901A city formally organized

The City of Manila is formally organized under the Philippine Commission during the American colonial period, laying the foundations of its modern local government.

1945Rising from the ruins

The Battle of Manila leaves the city devastated, but post-war reconstruction begins soon after, rebuilding landmarks and restoring civic life across the capital.

1975Seat of the National Capital Region

Manila becomes the seat of the newly created National Capital Region (Metro Manila), cementing its role as the country's center of government.

TodayA living heritage

Manila continues to modernize its services and infrastructure while preserving its heritage as the Pearl of the Orient for generations of Manileños to come.
