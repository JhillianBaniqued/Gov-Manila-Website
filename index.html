About the City Government of Manila

Learn about our mandate, guiding principles, and the story of the Pearl of the Orient.

Our Mission

Serbisyong Tapat, Manileño Bilang Prayoridad

To provide efficient, transparent, and responsive governance that uplifts the quality of life of every Manileño through inclusive public service, sustainable development, and the preservation of Manila's rich heritage for generations to come.

Our Vision

A World-Class, Livable, and Resilient Manila

A safe, healthy, and progressive capital city — globally competitive, culturally proud, and environmentally sustainable — where every resident enjoys equal access to opportunities, services, and a dignified quality of life.

What We Stand For

Our Story

A City of Heritage

1571Founding of Manila

Manila is established as the capital of the Spanish East Indies by Miguel López de Legazpi, becoming the seat of colonial government for over three centuries.

1901A city formally organized

The City of Manila is formally organized under the Philippine Commission during the American colonial period, laying the foundations of its modern local government.

1945Rising from the ruins

The Battle of Manila leaves the city devastated, but post-war reconstruction begins soon after, rebuilding landmarks and restoring civic life across the capital.

1975Seat of the National Capital Region

Manila becomes the seat of the newly created National Capital Region (Metro Manila), cementing its role as the country's center of government.

TodayA living heritage

Manila continues to modernize its services and infrastructure while preserving its heritage as the Pearl of the Orient for generations of Manileños to come.

    Privacy Notice

    How the City Government of Manila collects, uses, and protects your personal data.

    Effective Date: August 6, 2026  ·  Last Reviewed: August 6, 2026

    Tap on a section below to expand it and read the full details.

    On This Page 1Introduction 2Information We Collect 3How We Use Your Information 4Legal Basis for Processing 5Data Sharing & Disclosure 6Data Security 7Data Retention 8Your Rights 9Cookies & Tracking 10Children's Privacy 11Changes to This Notice 12Contact Us
    1

    Introduction

    The City Government of Manila ("the City," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of personal data entrusted to us by residents, visitors, employees, and other stakeholders. This Privacy Notice explains what personal information we collect through our offices, programs, and this website, why we collect it, how it is used and protected, and the rights available to you under Republic Act No. 10173, or the Data Privacy Act of 2012, its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and other applicable issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

    By transacting with the City Government of Manila, whether in person, through our website, or through our official mobile and digital channels, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this notice.

    2

    Information We Collect

    The type of information we collect depends on the service or transaction you are availing of. This may include:

    CategoryExamples
    Identity & Contact InformationFull name, address, date of birth, sex, civil status, contact number, email address, signature
    Government-Issued IDsVoter's ID, PhilSystem/PhilID, passport, driver's license, TIN, SSS/GSIS number, community tax certificate
    Transaction RecordsPermit and license applications, tax and payment records, complaint or feedback submissions, requests for public documents
    Employment InformationApplication details and employment records for job vacancies with the City Government
    Technical & Usage DataIP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and general location, collected automatically when you use this website
    Sensitive Personal InformationHealth-related data, biometric data, and similar information, collected only where required by specific programs or ordinances, and processed with additional safeguards
    3

    How We Use Your Information

    We use personal data for legitimate government purposes, including to:

    • Deliver services such as processing permits, licenses, certifications, and public assistance requests.
    • Administer programs including health, social welfare, disaster response, and livelihood initiatives.
    • Communicate with you regarding your applications, requests, complaints, or feedback.
    • Maintain public records as required by law, including civil registry and tax documentation.
    • Improve our services through website analytics and citizen feedback.
    • Comply with legal, audit, and reporting obligations to national government agencies and oversight bodies.
    • Ensure public safety and security, including responding to emergencies and hazards.
    4

    Legal Basis for Processing

    We process personal data on the basis of one or more of the following, consistent with the Data Privacy Act of 2012: your consent; the necessity of processing for the performance of a public function or the exercise of official authority vested in the City Government; compliance with a legal obligation; and, in the case of sensitive personal information, when necessary to protect your vital interests or as authorized by specific laws and ordinances.

    5

    Data Sharing & Disclosure

    Personal data collected by the City Government of Manila may be shared only under the following circumstances:

    • With other national or local government agencies where required by law or for the direct performance of their mandate (e.g., PSA, BIR, PhilHealth, SSS, DILG).
    • With authorized service providers and contractors who process data on our behalf, under strict confidentiality and data protection agreements.
    • When required by a valid court order, subpoena, or other lawful process.
    • In cases involving public health, safety, or emergency response, where disclosure is necessary to protect life or prevent harm.

    We do not sell or trade personal data to third parties for commercial or marketing purposes.

    6

    Data Security

    The City Government of Manila is registered with the National Privacy Commission and has implemented organizational, physical, and technical security measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include restricted access to records, secured storage of physical and electronic documents, and the designation of a Data Protection Officer who oversees our compliance with the Data Privacy Act.

    7

    Data Retention

    Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by the National Archives of the Philippines Act, the General Records Disposition Schedule, and other applicable laws. Once the retention period lapses and retention is no longer necessary, records are disposed of securely.

    8

    Your Rights

    Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to:

    • Be informed that your personal data will be, is being, or was processed.
    • Access your personal data held by the City Government, subject to certain exceptions.
    • Correct any inaccurate or outdated personal data.
    • Object to the processing of your data, where applicable.
    • Erasure or blocking of your data under conditions provided by law.
    • Data portability, where technically feasible.
    • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission if you believe your rights have been violated.

    To exercise any of these rights, please reach out through the contact details in Section 12 below.

    9

    Cookies & Tracking Technologies

    This website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, understand how the site is used, and improve overall functionality. You may disable cookies through your browser settings; however, some features of this website may not function properly as a result.

    10

    Children's Privacy

    Some City programs, such as those involving school-aged residents or social welfare beneficiaries, may require the collection of data belonging to minors. In such cases, we require the consent of a parent or legal guardian and apply additional safeguards appropriate for the protection of children's personal data.

    11

    Changes to This Notice

    We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, services, or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review this notice periodically.

    12

    Contact Us

    If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Notice or how your personal data is handled, you may reach our Data Protection Officer through the details below.
